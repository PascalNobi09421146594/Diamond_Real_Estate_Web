Description

This two-bedroom heritage apartment is located in the heart of Downtown, Yangon. This is a colonial apartment that has been renovated and designed with comfort in mind.


There is naturally exposed brickwork with teak wood throughout the entire living space. Natural light pours from sky windows in the upstairs bedroom, which is connected to a mezzanine. The stylish bathroom comes equipped with a large bathtub.

Leading downstairs is a spiral staircase that comes out onto an open-plan kitchen with an oven and dishwasher.


This apartment will come fully furnished, with a dishwasher, cleaning service and fibre internet, along with a backup generator.
